SUPPORT

Questions, bug reports and feature requests for BlackHole on iPhone and Mac.

Email john@orchestrsim.com with your device, OS version and what you saw. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I move around? Drag to orbit and pinch (or scroll on a Mac) to zoom. The sliders button opens the controls.

The frame rate is low. Choose Low or Medium quality in the controls. Ultra and Cinematic trace up to 500 steps per pixel and are meant for still frames and exports.

How do I restore Pro on a new device? Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Pro follows your Apple Account.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription? In Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions (iPhone), or in the App Store, your account, then Subscriptions (Mac). Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The Lifetime purchase never renews.

Mac: where did the window go? BlackHole keeps running in the menu bar. Choose Show Simulator Window from its menu bar icon, or press Command-1.

Photos permission (iPhone). BlackHole only asks to add images when you save a frame or export. It never reads your library.
