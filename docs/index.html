BLACKHOLE

A real-time general-relativity lab for iPhone and Mac, by Orch Aerospace, Inc.

BlackHole ray-traces a spinning (Kerr) black hole on your device's GPU. Every pixel is a light ray integrated backwards through curved spacetime, so the shadow, the lensed accretion disk and the photon ring change the moment you change the mass, the spin or the disk. Nothing is a pre-rendered video.

What you can do

  • Orbit and zoom around the hole, and set mass, spin, lensing, frame dragging and every accretion-disk property.
  • Read the event horizon, photon sphere and ISCO for the current spin, with the equations behind them and their literature sources.
  • Take eight guided lessons: the shadow, lensing, Doppler beaming, gravitational redshift, frame dragging, the innermost stable orbit, disk temperature and jets.
  • Load scenarios such as Sgr A*, M87*, Cygnus X-1 and a quasar.
  • iPhone: save a frame, or with Pro export a 4K poster or a 360° orbit video.
  • Mac: keep it in the menu bar, or with Pro run it as a live desktop wallpaper.
