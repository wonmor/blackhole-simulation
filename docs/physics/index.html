PHYSICS NOTES

What the app computes, and where each formula comes from.

Ray tracing

A Metal fragment shader integrates a null geodesic for every pixel of every frame in Kerr-Schild coordinates, which stay regular at the horizon. The step count runs from 32 (Low) to 500 (Cinematic); steps shrink near the photon sphere so the photon ring and higher-order images stay sharp. Frame dragging enters through the zero-angular-momentum-observer rate ω = 2Ma / (r³ + a²r).

Horizon, photon sphere, ISCO

The readouts use the closed forms of Bardeen, Press and Teukolsky (1972): the outer horizon r₊ = M + √(M² − a²), the prograde photon orbit, the innermost stable circular orbit and the equatorial ergosphere. On iPhone they come from gravitas, our Rust relativity library compiled into the app.

Accretion disk

The disk follows the Novikov–Thorne / Page–Thorne thin-disk temperature profile from the ISCO outwards. Each ray that hits the disk is coloured by a blackbody approximation at the local temperature and brightened or dimmed by the relativistic Doppler factor raised to the 3.5 power, which is why the approaching side is brighter.

Image pipeline

Rendering is HDR with temporal anti-aliasing, bloom and ACES tone mapping. Posters on iPhone are rendered at 4K with 16-pass supersampling.
